# Dresso — Virtual Fashion Try-On

**Your style. Your avatar. Your fit.**

## 1. Project Overview

Dresso is an AI-powered virtual fashion try-on platform designed for boutiques, independent fashion brands, and online clothing sellers.

Many customers hesitate to purchase clothes online because they cannot try them on beforehand. Meanwhile, small fashion businesses often lack access to the expensive virtual try-on technology available to larger retailers.

Dresso aims to bridge this gap by allowing customers to explore garments on personalized, adjustable 3D avatars, helping them visualize outfits before purchasing.

Our goal is to make virtual try-on more accessible to independent fashion businesses while creating a more personalized online shopping experience.

## 2. Idea and Key Features

- **Personalized 3D avatars:** Adjustable body shapes for a more personalized experience.
- **Virtual garment try-on:** Preview digital garments on a 3D avatar.
- **3D garment digitization:** Prepare clothing models and textures for virtual visualization.
- **Mobile-first experience:** Designed around an Android application.
- **Privacy-conscious design:** Aim to keep personal body measurements on the user's device wherever possible.
- **Boutique-friendly platform:** Help independent sellers showcase their collections digitally.

## 3. Technology Stack

| Technology | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Kotlin | Android application development |
| Blender | 3D modeling, avatar rigging, and garment preparation |
| GLB / glTF | 3D asset interchange and rendering |
| Filament | Planned real-time 3D rendering on Android |
| 3D skeletal rigging and morph targets | Avatar movement and body-shape adjustment |

### Current Development Status

The project includes a rigged 3D human avatar, adjustable body-shape controls, and validated GLB export. Automated garment generation, realistic garment fitting, texture accuracy, and the complete mobile try-on workflow are ongoing development priorities.

## 4. Product Demo



The demo showcases the current prototype, 3D avatar, garment visualization workflow, and planned virtual try-on experience.

## 5. Roadmap

- Improve 3D garment generation and asset preparation.
- Refine garment fitting across different avatar body shapes.
- Improve fabric textures, colors, and material realism.
- Integrate and optimize the 3D rendering workflow in Android.
- Pilot the platform with independent boutiques and fashion brands.

## 6. Business Model

Dresso plans to generate revenue through monthly platform subscriptions for fashion businesses, per-garment 3D digitization fees, and a proposed commission on completed sales. The customer-facing virtual try-on experience is intended to remain free.

## 7. Vision

We believe every boutique should have the opportunity to offer a personalized digital fitting room, not just large fashion retailers.

**Dresso — Bringing the fitting room to online fashion.**
